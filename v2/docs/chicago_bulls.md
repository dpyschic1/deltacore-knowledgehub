# Chicago Bulls

## Franchise History

The Chicago Bulls were founded on January 16, 1966. The team has won six
NBA championships, all during the 1990s: 1991, 1992, 1993, 1996, 1997, and
1998.

## First Three-Peat (1991-1993)

The core roster during the first three-peat was Michael Jordan, Scottie
Pippen, and Horace Grant, coached by Phil Jackson.

## Second Three-Peat (1996-1998)

The core roster during the second three-peat was Michael Jordan, Scottie
Pippen, and Dennis Rodman, coached by Phil Jackson. The 1995-96 team set an
NBA record with 72 regular-season wins, a record that stood until the
2015-16 season.

## Draft History

Notable draft picks in franchise history include Michael Jordan (1984, 3rd
overall), Scottie Pippen (1987, 5th overall, acquired via draft-night
trade), and Derrick Rose (2008, 1st overall).

## Retired Numbers

The Chicago Bulls have retired five jersey numbers: 1 (Derrick Rose), 4
(Jerry Sloan), 10 (Bob Love), 23 (Michael Jordan), and 33 (Scottie Pippen).

## Arenas

The Bulls played at the International Amphitheatre and Chicago Stadium
before moving to the United Center in 1994.
