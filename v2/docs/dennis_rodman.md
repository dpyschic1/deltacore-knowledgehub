# Dennis Rodman

## Background

Dennis Rodman played college basketball at Southeastern Oklahoma State
University from 1983 to 1986, where he was a three-time NAIA All-American.
He was selected 27th overall in the 1986 NBA draft by the Detroit Pistons.

## NBA Career

Rodman played for the Detroit Pistons from 1986 to 1993, the San Antonio
Spurs from 1993 to 1995, the Chicago Bulls from 1995 to 1998, the Los
Angeles Lakers in 1999, and the Dallas Mavericks in 2000. His head coach in
Detroit was Chuck Daly; his head coach in San Antonio was Bob Hill; his
head coach in Chicago was Phil Jackson.

## Championships

Rodman won five NBA championships: 1989 and 1990 with the Detroit Pistons
under head coach Chuck Daly, and 1996, 1997, and 1998 with the Chicago
Bulls under head coach Phil Jackson.

## Awards

Rodman led the NBA in rebounding seven consecutive seasons, from 1992 to
1998. He won the NBA Defensive Player of the Year award in 1990 and 1991,
and was named to the All-Defensive First Team seven times.
