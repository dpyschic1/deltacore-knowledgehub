# Michael Jordan

## Background

Michael Jordan attended the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
from 1981 to 1984. He was selected third overall in the 1984 NBA draft by
the Chicago Bulls.

## NBA Career

Jordan played for the Chicago Bulls from 1984 to 1993, and again from 1995
to 1998. He also played for the Washington Wizards from 2001 to 2003. His
head coaches with the Bulls were Doug Collins and later Phil Jackson.

## Championships

Jordan won six NBA championships with the Chicago Bulls: 1991, 1992, and
1993 during the first three-peat, and 1996, 1997, and 1998 during the
second three-peat. He was named Finals MVP in all six of those series.

## Awards

Jordan won five NBA Most Valuable Player awards, the 1988 Defensive Player
of the Year award, and the 1985 Rookie of the Year award. He led the league
in scoring ten times.
