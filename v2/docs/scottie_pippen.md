# Scottie Pippen

## Background

Scottie Pippen attended the University of Central Arkansas from 1983 to
1987. He was selected fifth overall in the 1987 NBA draft by the Seattle
SuperSonics, and was traded to the Chicago Bulls on draft night in exchange
for the eighth pick, Olden Polynice.

## NBA Career

Pippen played for the Chicago Bulls from 1987 to 1998, the Houston Rockets
in 1999, the Portland Trail Blazers from 1999 to 2003, and returned to the
Chicago Bulls from 2003 to 2004. His head coach for all six of his
championship seasons in Chicago was Phil Jackson.

## Championships

Pippen won six NBA championships, all with the Chicago Bulls: 1991, 1992,
1993, 1996, 1997, and 1998. He did not win a championship with Houston or
Portland.

## Awards

Pippen was a seven-time NBA All-Star and was named Most Valuable Player of
the 1994 All-Star Game. He was selected to the NBA All-Defensive First Team
eight times and won two Olympic gold medals, in 1992 and 1996. The Chicago
Bulls retired his jersey number, 33.
